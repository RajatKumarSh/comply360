# Comply360

## Workshop Lifecycle, Audit, Compliance & Operations Management Platform

Comply360 is a scalable platform designed to manage the complete lifecycle of workshops — from LOI issuance and Pre-COB readiness through operational audits, compliance management, manpower, training, assets, expenses and historical reporting.

## Core Principles

- Workshop is the central entity.
- Historical records are never overwritten.
- Audits are immutable snapshots.
- Compliance updates create new history rather than modifying past audits.
- Audit questions, scoring, criticality and conditional logic should be configurable.
- Every important action should be traceable through an audit trail.
- The system should be scalable and deployable independently of Google Forms/Sheets.
